**Summary:** Presents a structure-aware hybrid model that injects contact-map priors and residue-level constraints into sequence-based predictors to maintain interpretability with limited labeled isolates. Across nine *Mycobacterium tuberculosis* resistance genes, the Fused Ridge model reaches a mean AUC of 0.766, versus 0.755 for plain ridge regression and 0.603 for zero-shot ESM-2 scoring.
