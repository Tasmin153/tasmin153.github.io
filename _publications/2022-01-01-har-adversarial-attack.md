Compares Keras-LSTM, RNN-LSTM, CNN, and ResNet classifiers on a feature-engineered human-activity-recognition dataset, with ResNet reaching 99.9% accuracy. Also evaluates model robustness under FGSM and Basic Iterative Method adversarial attacks, highlighting security risks in HAR deployment pipelines that had not previously been studied for this dataset and model family.
