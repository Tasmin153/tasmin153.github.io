Studies natural convection of a Cu–water nanofluid, modeled as non-Newtonian via a power-law and Brinkman-extended Darcy formulation, inside a two-dimensional wavy porous cavity. Uses the finite volume method to characterize how the power-law index, Darcy number, volume fraction, and Rayleigh number affect heat transfer and local entropy generation.
